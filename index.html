Our commitment to brands

What you can expect from us, in writing.

Price integrity

We follow your minimum advertised price (MAP) policy. We never dump your product or compete against your distributors.

Brand protection

On Amazon we sell on your official listings: no duplicate listings, no changes to your content, and full respect for your Brand Registry.

Authentic products, handled with care

We buy directly from the brand or its authorized distributor. We store and ship in original, unaltered packaging.

Clear, on-time payments

We work with prepayment or the terms we agree on. Organized invoicing and direct communication.

Supplier questions

What brands usually ask us.

Are you a legally registered U.S. company?

Yes. Lunita Tucumana is an LLC registered in the State of Florida, with a federal EIN and a valid resale certificate. We'll send you the documents whenever you ask.

Do you follow our pricing policy?

Always. We sign your MAP or distribution agreement and apply it across all our channels.

How do you protect our brand on Amazon?

We sell on your official listings, follow your MAP and Brand Registry, and never list anything without your authorization. If you'd like to limit products or regions, we agree on it in writing before the first order.

How do you handle payments?

We can start with prepayment and, as the relationship grows, discuss credit terms under your policies.
